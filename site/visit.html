Good to know

  • Do we need a reservation?

    +

    Walk in any time we're open and pick a project. Birthday parties and group bookings are reserved ahead — weekends fill up first.

  • How does pricing work?

    +

    You pay per project, so you only pay for what you actually make. Birthday party pricing is per child and listed on the Birthday Parties page.

  • Is Lulu Land just for girls?

    +

    No. The Sports Club sits right next to the Decoden table on purpose. Same creativity, different passions — everyone belongs here.

  • Can we take our projects home?

    +

    Yes. Every experience includes materials and a take-home creation. A few pieces need to set overnight — we'll tell you at the table.

  • Where exactly are you?

    +

    Westfield Topanga, second floor, in Canoga Park. Take the escalator up from center court.
