A different kind of party

Lulu Land is a creative kids' studio, party space, and celebration destination where kids get to make, personalize, decorate, and take home something of their own.

Our birthday parties are designed to feel different from a traditional play-place party. Kids take part in hands-on creative experiences, hosted by the Lulu Land team.

Keep the celebration simple with one creative activity, or build a larger experience with multiple activities, food, goodie bags, custom décor, entertainment, beauty services and photography.

  • ✦Reserved party area for your group
  • ✦Dedicated party host
  • ✦Full access to your selected creative experiences
  • ✦Clean, safe & welcoming environment
  • ✦Music to keep the party going
  • ✦Amazing memories
